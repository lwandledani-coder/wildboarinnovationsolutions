About WILDBOAR

WILDBOAR INNOVATION SOLUTIONS (PTY) LTD is a proudly South African technology company dedicated to delivering cutting-edge digital solutions and reliable technical support services.

Founded with a vision to bridge the gap between innovation and accessibility, we specialize in crafting custom e-commerce applications, professional web portfolios, and comprehensive technical support across Windows, Linux, and macOS environments. Our hardware repair services ensure your devices stay in peak condition, while our journal creation service transforms your content into polished PDF and Word documents.

At WILDBOAR I S, we don't just solve problems, we forge lasting partnerships built on trust, excellence, and a relentless drive to innovate. Whether you're an entrepreneur launching your first online store or an established enterprise seeking robust CRM solutions, we have the expertise to elevate your digital presence.

Mission & Vision

To empower businesses and individuals by delivering innovative, high-quality technology solutions, from robust e-commerce platforms and captivating web portfolios to dependable technical support and hardware repair services. We are committed to excellence, integrity, and continuous improvement in everything we do, ensuring our clients thrive in an ever-evolving digital landscape.

  • Deliver exceptional digital solutions tailored to client needs
  • Provide reliable, accessible technical support across all major platforms
  • Foster long-term partnerships through trust and transparency

Our Vision

We envision a future where every entrepreneur, regardless of size or budget, has access to world-class digital tools, expert technical guidance, and the support they need to compete and succeed on the global stage.

  • Be the go-to innovation partner across the African continent

  • Champion digital inclusion and technological empowerment
